Popular Dog Walking Parks & Beaches

South Wales offers some fantastic locations for dog walking. I regularly walk dogs in and around these popular parks, forests, beaches, and scenic routes:

  • Afan Forest Park – Woodland trails and scenic mountain paths
  • Margam Park – Open parkland and woodland walks
  • Neath Canal Towpath – Flat, peaceful canal-side walks
  • Cilfrew Woods – Quiet woodland walks near Neath
  • Aberavon Beach – Wide sandy beach perfect for long walks
  • Swansea Bay Beach – Popular seafront dog walks
  • Mumbles Coastal Path – Beautiful sea views and cliff paths
  • Bracelet Bay – Scenic coastal dog-friendly areas
  • Rhossili Bay – Stunning National Trust beach walks
  • Penllegaer Woods – Forest tracks and shaded trails
  • Waterfall Country (Pontneddfechan) – Nature walks and waterfalls
  • Llangorse Lake – Lakeside paths and open countryside

These locations are ideal for energetic dogs who love exploring, as well as relaxed scenic walks for older dogs. I always ensure dogs are walked safely, responsibly, and in accordance with local regulations.
