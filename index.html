Welcome to Julie’s Pet Services

At Julie’s Pet Services, we provide expert mobile pet ultrasound scanning, microchipping, dog walking, and dog sitting services across South Wales. If your pets require flea treatment, we can also treat your home to ensure a safe and comfortable environment for everyone.

Pet Scanning Services

Our state-of-the-art ultrasound technology offers a non-invasive, accurate way to monitor your pet’s health and confirm pregnancies. Whether you’re expecting new puppies or kittens, our mobile scanning service ensures your pet stays comfortable at home, stress-free and relaxed.

Microchipping Services

Ensure your pet’s safety with our professional, fully qualified microchipping services. It's a legal requirement for dogs in the UK. Our stress-free process gives peace of mind knowing your pet can be quickly identified and reunited if lost.

Flea Treatments & Home Care

If your pets need flea treatment, we can arrange a professional treatment for your home as well through our partnership with Welsh Town and Country Pest Services. This ensures your pets and living space remain healthy, safe, and flea-free.
