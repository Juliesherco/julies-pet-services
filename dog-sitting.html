Professional Dog Sitting & Home Visits

At Julie’s Pet Services, we provide reliable, loving dog sitting through flexible home visits in Neath, Port Talbot, Swansea, Aberdare and surrounding areas. Whether you’re at work, on holiday or just need help during the day, we come to your home so your dog stays in their familiar environment — happy, safe and relaxed.

What’s Included in Our Dog Sitting Visits

  • Feeding & fresh water refills
  • Playtime, cuddles & mental stimulation
  • Walks upon request (tailored to your dog’s energy & needs)
  • Medication administration if required
  • Toilet breaks & clean-up
  • Daily photo/text updates for peace of mind
  • 24/7 emergency contact availability

Visits can be scheduled once or multiple times per day — fully customised to your schedule and your dog’s routine.

Pricing

Dog sitting visits start from £30 per visit (30–45 minutes). Additional walks, longer visits or multiple pets can be added for a personalised package. Contact us for an accurate quote based on your needs.
