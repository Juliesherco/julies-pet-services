Professional Pet Microchipping Services

At Julie’s Pet Services, I provide safe, quick and fully compliant microchipping for dogs, cats and other pets across Neath, Port Talbot, Swansea, Aberdare and surrounding areas in South Wales. Microchipping is a legal requirement for all dogs in the UK (from 8 weeks old), and strongly recommended for cats and other pets. My mobile service comes to you — no stressful trips to the vet.

The process takes just a few minutes: a tiny chip (the size of a grain of rice) is inserted under the skin between the shoulder blades using a sterile needle. It’s virtually painless, and once registered, your contact details are linked to the chip in national databases. If your pet ever gets lost, any vet, rescue or scanner can quickly identify them and reunite you.

  • Stress-free at-home service
  • Immediate registration with national database
  • Compliance with UK pet identification laws
