Mobile Pet Ultrasound Scanning Services

Julie’s Pet Scanning provides professional, non-invasive ultrasound services directly at your home in Neath, Port Talbot, Swansea, Aberdare and surrounding areas. Whether monitoring pregnancy, checking general health or assessing organ function, our mobile scans are stress-free, accurate and gentle for your pet.

Accurate & Comfortable Ultrasound

Using high-quality portable ultrasound equipment, we deliver clear, reliable images with minimal handling. Ideal for confirming pregnancies, monitoring fetal development, or routine wellness checks — all in your pet’s familiar environment.

  • No sedation required in most cases
  • Quick 15–30 minute sessions
  • Immediate verbal results & images
  • Follow-up advice included

Pregnancy Scanning & Monitoring

Confirm pregnancy from around day 25–30, count puppies/kittens, and track development safely at home. Perfect for breeders, owners, or anyone wanting peace of mind about their pet’s pregnancy journey.

  • Early detection & viability checks
  • Heart rate & growth monitoring
  • Guidance on whelping/queening prep

Specialist Pre-Whelping Care

Protecting your newborn litter is vital. Fleas can cause severe health issues for puppies and kittens. I recommend a professional, pet-safe home treatment before your litter arrives to ensure a parasite-free nesting environment.

If you want your house treated for fleas before your puppies arrive, please ask Julie to book you in with Welsh Town and Country Pest Services for a specialist treatment.
