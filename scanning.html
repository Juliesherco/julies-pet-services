Mobile Pet Scanning Services

Julie’s Pet Scanning & Microchipping offers professional, mobile pet ultrasound services across Neath, Port Talbot, Swansea, and Aberdare. Our non-invasive scans provide accurate insights into your pet’s health and pregnancy status, all from the comfort of your home.

Accurate & Stress-Free Ultrasound

Using advanced ultrasound technology, I provide safe and precise scanning for pets. Perfect for monitoring pregnancies, checking organ health, or routine wellness checks, all conducted with minimal stress for your pet.

Mobile Service at Your Door

Our fully mobile service brings professional scanning equipment to your home, creating a calm and familiar environment for your pet. This ensures a stress-free experience while delivering detailed and accurate results.

Pregnancy Monitoring & Health Checks

Whether you are expecting puppies or kittens, or require general health monitoring, I provide vital information and guidance on your pet’s well-being, with care, precision, and professionalism.

Partnered with Welsh Town & Country Pest Services

To ensure a safe home environment for your pets, Julie’s Pet Scanning partners with Welsh Town and Country Pest Services. Their pet-friendly pest control complements our services, keeping your pets protected both at home and during visits.
